tags: [content, editorial, marketing]
---

Write the prose as plain paragraphs inside `ContentSectionBody`; a `<strong>` phrase is lifted
to full contrast.

- `split` sets `ContentSectionMedia` beside the prose; `stacked` centres the prose over it.
- Add a `ContentSectionQuote` at the end of the body for a pull quote.
