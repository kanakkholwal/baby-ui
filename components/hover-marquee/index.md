---
title: Hover Marquee
description: "Truncated text that slides to reveal the rest on hover or focus."
component: hover-marquee
category: text
tags: [truncate, ellipsis, marquee, hover, sidebar]
---

Use it where one line is all the space there is: chat titles, sidebar rows, headers. The slide
runs at a steady speed, so a long title takes longer than a short one. With reduced motion the
text keeps its ellipsis and shows the full value as a native tooltip.
