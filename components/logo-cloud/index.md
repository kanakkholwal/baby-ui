---
title: Logo Cloud
description: "Customer logos as a centred grid or a ruled row; hovering blurs them and surfaces a link to every customer story."
component: logo-cloud
category: blocks
tags: [logos, social proof, marketing]
---

Put one `LogoCloudItem` per logo in a `LogoCloudList`, inside `LogoCloudContent` with an optional
`LogoCloudLink`.

- With a mouse the link waits over the blurred logos; on touch it sits underneath.
- Leave the link out for a plain, still row of logos.
