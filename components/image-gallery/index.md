---
title: Image Gallery
description: "Responsive picture gallery that keeps each picture's ratio, with hover info and selection."
component: image-gallery
category: advanced
tags: [gallery, masonry, images, selection]
---

Give `ImageGallery` your pictures, render them with `ImageGalleryGrid`, and handle `onOpen` to
show one in an Image Viewer. Turn on `selectable` for a selection mode where a click toggles.
Put an `EmptyHeader` and a call to action inside `ImageGalleryEmpty` for the no-pictures state.
