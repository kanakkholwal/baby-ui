---
title: Integration Section
description: "The tools a product connects to, as cards with a line of copy and a link, or a cloud of logo tiles around your own mark."
component: integration-section
category: blocks
tags: [integrations, landing, marketing]
---

One `IntegrationSectionItem` per tool: a logo, a name, a line of copy and a link.

- `grid` shows every part as a card; `cluster` shows the logo as a tile and makes the whole
  tile the link.
- Mark your own product `featured` and put it in the middle of the list.
- Give each link an `aria-label` naming the tool, e.g. "Learn more about Stripe".
