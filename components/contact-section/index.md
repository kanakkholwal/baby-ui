List each way to reach you as a `ContactSectionChannel`, then put your own fields and submit
button in `ContactSectionForm` and handle its submit.

- `split` sets the form beside the copy; `stacked` centres the header and form.
- Compose the fields from Field, Input, NativeSelect and Textarea.
