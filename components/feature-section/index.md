---
title: Feature Section
description: "A product's features as cards, ruled cells, open columns, or a list beside a visual, each item an icon, a title and a line of copy."
component: feature-section
category: blocks
tags: [features, landing, marketing]
---

Put the heading in `FeatureSectionHeader` and one `FeatureSectionItem` per feature in
`FeatureSectionList`.

- `cards` and `divided` centre the header; `plain` and `split` keep it left.
- In `split`, add a `FeatureSectionMedia` after the list; it sits on the right from 56rem.
- Pass each icon as an inline `<svg>`; it inherits the variant's colour and size.
