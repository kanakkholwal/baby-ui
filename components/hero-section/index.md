---
title: Hero Section
description: "A landing hero with an announcement pill, headline, actions and customer logos, over or beside a framed or tilted product shot."
component: hero-section
category: blocks
tags: [hero, landing, marketing]
---

Compose the copy in `HeroSectionContent`, then put a product shot in `HeroSectionMedia`.

- `centered` stacks the copy over the media; `split` sets them side by side.
- `HeroSectionMedia` takes `plain`, `framed` (a rim that fades out at the bottom) or `tilted`.
- `HeroSectionLogos` dims the other logos while one is hovered.
