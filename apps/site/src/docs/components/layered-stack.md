---
title: Layered Stack
description: Image cards piled in the centre at loose angles that fan out into a grid on hover or focus.
component: layered-stack
category: animated
tags: [stack, cards, hover, grid, images]
---

Cards rest in a loose pile and spread into their grid cells on hover or keyboard focus, staggered
in order. Arrow keys move between cards, Enter toggles and Escape stacks them again.

Offsets are measured, the motion is plain CSS transitions. `open` is controllable with
`onOpenChange`, or `bind:open` in Svelte.
