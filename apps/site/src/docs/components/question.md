---
title: Question
description: Inline clarifying question with single or multiple choice answers.
component: question
category: agents
tags: [question]
---

Single choice answers on click. Multiple choice collects and waits for an explicit submit,
because there is no way to know when someone has finished picking.

Once answered, the unchosen options are disabled rather than removed. Removing them leaves
a bare answer with no visible question, which reads as a non-sequitur when you scroll back
through the transcript.
