---
title: Wave Reveal
description: Reveals letters or words one by one with a wave effect and an optional blur.
component: wave-reveal
category: text
tags: [text, wave, reveal, stagger]
---

`mode="letter"` staggers per character; `mode="word"` staggers per whole word for a
calmer read on longer copy. `blur` adds a blur-to-sharp resolve on top of the fade/drift.
