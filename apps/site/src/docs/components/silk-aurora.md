title: Silk Aurora
description: Three soft silk ribbons with a pearlescent sheen, drawn in WebGL from theme tokens.
component: silk-aurora
category: backgrounds
tags: [background, aurora, silk, webgl]
---

Three sine ribbons are warped by noise and layered over a soft atmosphere, with a pearlescent band and fine glints in the sheen colour. The pointer pulls the ribbons slightly and lifts a glow under it.

The root fills its nearest positioned parent (or the viewport with `position="fixed"`) and renders `children` above the effect. The loop pauses off screen and in hidden tabs, reduced motion draws one still frame, and browsers without WebGL get a token gradient.
