---
title: Pagination
description: Page links with a sliding window and always-visible first and last.
component: pagination
category: base
tags: [pagination]
---

First and last are always present; `siblings` controls how many pages sit either side of
the current one. Page 1 and the final page are the two people actually jump to.

Both arrows carry accessible names. A chevron with no name is announced as "button", which
in a row of five buttons is useless.
